# The Clockmaker's Last Hour
## Story contract and production brief

## Non-negotiable lesson from Bao and Lighthouse

Do not build a generic clock workshop and decorate it six times.

Every chapter must be an authored reversible film sequence:

```text
apprentice objective
→ physical contact with a time object
→ mechanical obstacle
→ camera choreography
→ city/world response
→ time-state consequence
```

The scene passes only if a reader can answer:

1. Where is the apprentice?
2. What are they trying to repair or prevent?
3. What does the clockwork world do back?
4. What changed because they acted?

## Story premise

A young apprentice finds an unspent hour sealed inside their late mentor's master clock. Believing that repairing the city’s broken clocks can delay the mentor’s final goodbye, they race through a clockwork city. At the final hour, they choose to let the hour pass and use what they learned to start a new clock.

## Hero, narrator, antagonist, transformation

| Role | Design |
|---|---|
| Hero | Young apprentice: compact silhouette, rolled sleeves, blue work coat, brass keyring, red thread/spool, pocket tools. |
| Narrator | Mentor’s inherited pocket watch. Warm, precise, gently rueful; it hears every second and understands what an hour costs. |
| Antagonistic force | Stopped/discordant time, represented by the Unspent Hour: an ink-blue clock face with a missing minute hand. |
| Central prop | Pocket watch: compass, light source, key, and emotional record. |
| Transformation object | Master clock: starts as a sealed hour, becomes a new living civic clock. |
| Material language | Oxidized brass, enamel, ink-blue night, blueprint paper, red thread, ivory clock faces, warm mechanical light. |

## Visual rules

- No generic steampunk clutter. Every gear, spring, key, pendulum, or blueprint must serve a contact point, a route, a clock state, or a visual transition.
- Time reversal is **material reconstruction**, not a reverse-filter effect: broken teeth rejoin, paper folds flatten, dropped tools rise, cracked enamel heals, city windows relight in sequence.
- The city is a tabletop clockwork model at first, then becomes traversable at human scale in Chapter III.
- The pocket watch is always visually legible: warm brass/gold against blue-black time.
- Red thread is the visual route through the story; it becomes the final minute hand.

# Six chapter production map

## I. The Workshop Wakes — **Wind the first spring**

**Spatial proposition:** intimate desk-scale workshop; the camera moves through parts large enough to feel architectural.

```text
apprentice wakes a dormant workbench
→ winds pocket watch
→ blueprint lines rise from paper
→ unfinished clocks awaken one by one
→ master clock reveals the sealed hour
```

| Local progress | Camera | Action | Consequence |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 0.00–0.18 | Macro glide over tools, springs, dust | Pocket watch ticks once | warm light traces blueprint edges |
| 0.18–0.48 | Over shoulder at master clock | Apprentice fits a key and winds | clocks around bench wake at different speeds |
| 0.48–0.76 | Circular desk orbit | Opens master clock face | missing minute hand and sealed hour revealed |
| 0.76–1.00 | Pull through a blueprint | Apprentice follows red thread route | drawing folds into city transition |

**Hero interaction:** key/hand anchor to winding crown.

**Exit transition:** blueprint paper folds upward until it becomes the city map.

---

## II. The City Disagrees — **Set the clocks in order**

**Spatial proposition:** a miniature clockwork city viewed from above, then entered along a street of clock faces.

```text
apprentice sets street clocks
→ each one runs at a conflicting tempo
→ bridges and train routes misalign
→ red thread reveals a route to the gear tower
```

| Local progress | Camera | Action | Consequence |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 0.00–0.24 | Overhead city model | Apprentice moves between clock squares | hands spin out of sync |
| 0.24–0.52 | Low route-follow shot | Repairs a station clock | a tiny train resumes, bridge teeth align |
| 0.52–0.78 | Lateral street chase | Chases a loose minute hand | city blocks fold / routes misalign |
| 0.78–1.00 | Tilt to vertical tower | Thread points up through gear stack | city becomes gear-tower transition |

**Hero interaction:** tool/key anchors to clock crowns and a loose minute hand.

**Exit transition:** the city’s map grid rotates ninety degrees and becomes tower gears.

---

## III. The Impossible Gear Tower — **Climb before the minute falls**

**Spatial proposition:** vertical, rotating gear tower; all ascent choices are timing problems.

```text
apprentice runs across teeth
→ catches a moving pendulum
→ climbs rotating gears
→ reaches the unspent hour chamber
```

| Local progress | Camera | Action | Consequence |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 0.00–0.28 | Side-on run | Apprentice crosses gear teeth | each tooth locks behind them |
| 0.28–0.56 | Low pendulum swing | Catches pendulum arm | camera swings / tower rotates |
| 0.56–0.82 | Ascending spiral | Climbs interlocking gears | city lights flicker below |
| 0.82–1.00 | Chamber close-up | Reaches sealed hour | minute hand begins to fracture |

**Hero interaction:** foot anchors to gear teeth, hand anchor to pendulum, body force against rotation.

**Exit transition:** a fractured minute hand breaks into blueprint fragments.

---

## IV. The Remembered Hour — **Let the past rebuild**

**Spatial proposition:** mentor’s workshop memory, reconstructed from reversed objects.

```text
apprentice tries to reverse the hour
→ tools rise, broken clock heals, mentor memory appears
→ the memory cannot be held
→ apprentice recognizes the cost of stopping time
```

| Local progress | Camera | Action | Consequence |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 0.00–0.25 | Reverse fall through fragments | Broken parts fly upward | workshop reconstructs around hero |
| 0.25–0.58 | Intimate workbench two-shot | Apprentice and mentor memory repair clock | the whole room runs backward beautifully |
| 0.58–0.82 | Close pocket-watch insert | Apprentice tries to stop hand | mentor fades as clock resists |
| 0.82–1.00 | Wide room collapse | Reconstructed past unbuilds | only red thread remains |

**Hero interaction:** hand anchor holds pocket watch; mentor remains a light/paper echo, never a fully physical resurrected character.

**Exit transition:** reversing fragments converge into a single red thread.

---

## V. The Final Hour — **Choose to let it pass**

**Spatial proposition:** master clock chamber, calm and monumental after the chase.

```text
apprentice stands before unspent hour
→ releases the stopped hand
→ lets final minute complete
→ threads a new red minute hand into place
```

| Local progress | Camera | Action | Consequence |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 0.00–0.30 | Quiet approach | Apprentice carries red thread | machinery waits, silent |
| 0.30–0.62 | Face-and-hand insert | Releases sealed hand | final hour begins moving |
| 0.62–0.84 | Orbital clock face | Threads red minute hand | brass and enamel reassemble |
| 0.84–1.00 | Beam through dial | Clock strikes once | city receives warm time transition |

**Hero interaction:** two-handed thread placement; pocket watch closes only after final hand moves.

**Exit transition:** clock dial expands to sunrise over city.

---

## VI. A New Clock Ticks — **Make time together**

**Spatial proposition:** restored city at dawn; model and real city now coexist.

```text
new civic clock begins ticking
→ repaired routes move in sequence
→ apprentice leaves mentor’s watch on workbench
→ city carries the rhythm forward
```

| Local progress | Camera | Action | Consequence |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 0.00–0.28 | Close master clock | First new tick | city clocks synchronize outward |
| 0.28–0.58 | Pull through model streets | Apprentice watches | train, bridge, lights, pendulums resume |
| 0.58–0.84 | Workshop return | Places pocket watch beside blueprint | mentor’s memory becomes a small warm reflection |
| 0.84–1.00 | Aerial city pullback | Apprentice exits workshop | new clock keeps time without them |

**Hero interaction:** set pocket watch down; no triumph pose. The emotional resolution is continuation.

# Shared production systems

## Apprentice rig

Required pose/action API:

```text
idle
walk
run
windKey
repair
catchPendulum
climbGear
holdWatch
reachThread
placeHand
release
setWatchDown
```

Required anchors:

```text
leftHand
rightHand
leftFoot
rightFoot
pocketWatchAnchor
keyAnchor
threadAnchor
```

## Time reversal system

A reusable `TimeReversalSystem` must support:

```text
capture object rest state
capture broken/scattered state
reconstruct using local progress
reverse exactly on upward scroll
apply to meshes, paper fragments, glyphs, lights, and clock hands
```

It must not reverse global elapsed time. Ambient loops continue gently; authored object state is the reversible layer.

## Clockwork kit

Build reusable factories before scenes:

```text
gear ring / gear tooth path
clock face / hands / numerals
pendulum / escapement
winding crown / key
spring / coil
blueprint sheet / folding line
brass housing
enamel dial
red thread path
miniature building / train / bridge / street clock
```

## Narration model

Use the chapter-addressed narration controller already built for Lighthouse and Bao:

```text
enter chapter
→ stop prior line
→ start current chapter line
→ caption appears
→ ambient ducks
→ line ends without repeating in same chapter
```

The pocket watch narrates. Its voice should be distinct from the Lighthouse narrator: precise, intimate, less weathered, with a quiet mechanical resonance.

# Production order

1. **Visual bible** — apprentice turnaround, clockwork material board, six keyframes.
2. **Benchmark Chapter IV** — remembered-hour reconstruction proves time reversal, mentor memory, pocket-watch contact, and emotional intimacy.
3. **Gear Tower** — proves movement, foot/hand contacts, vertical camera, timing.
4. **Final Hour** — proves thread placement and mechanical transformation.
5. **Workshop / City / Dawn** — establish world, restore civic consequences.
6. **Narration, audio, captions, transcript, smoke screenshots, budgets.**

# Quality gates

A chapter cannot be accepted if it is only:

```text
clock props + blue lighting + spinning gears
```

It must have:

- a unique spatial proposition;
- one clear apprentice verb;
- a contact constraint;
- a mechanical response;
- a reversible reconstruction/destruction state;
- a camera contact insert;
- a consequence that motivates the next chapter.
