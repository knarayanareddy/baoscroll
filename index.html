The Lighthouse That Remembered Names

On this coast, the sea takes one memory from every departing sailor. An old keeper records each name so no one is lost twice.

I. Harbor at Dusk

The keeper walks the familiar dock with lantern and book. He checks the mooring line, watches the village windows answer the evening, and climbs to the light as a dark shape gathers beyond the harbor.

II. The First Name

In the lantern room, Elias Rune disappears from the Keeping Book. The keeper reaches for the wet ink as it lifts from the page and passes through the rain-streaked glass toward the sea.

III. The Storm Wall

The sea arrives as a wall of black water. The keeper hauls the boat line, braces against the wind, and runs as the dock floods beneath the broken beam.

IV. What the Water Kept

Beneath the surface, the keeper enters a wreck filled with letters, relics, and names the water could not consume. Each recovered memory gathers in the lantern and rises toward the lighthouse.

V. The Lamp Rekindled

The keeper climbs the spiral stair and turns the brass wheel of the Fresnel lamp. Names enter the core, its glass aligns, and a beam opens the storm.

VI. Birds of Morning

The keeper releases the names. They leave the beam as birds and fly across the waking harbor. The book remains, but its names have returned to the living world.
